Veterinary clinic - owners, animals and treatments
==================================================

Views
-----
- index.html       - home, owners with the date of the oldest vaccination (due vaccinations)
- owners.html      - owner list (each row opens the owner, plus an add button)
- owner.html       - one owner: editable details, delete, animals list, add animal
- owner-new.html   - create a new owner (empty form)
- animals.html     - animal list (each row opens the animal)
- animal.html      - one animal: editable details, delete, treatments (add / edit)
- animal-new.html  - create a new animal (owner preset)

Routing
-------

common middleware/
    requireOption.js
    renderMW.js

middleware/owner/
    getDueVaccinationsMW.js
    getOwnersMW.js
    getOwnerMW.js
    saveOwnerMW.js
    delOwnerMW.js

middleware/animal/
    getAnimalsMW.js
    getAnimalMW.js
    saveAnimalMW.js
    delAnimalMW.js

middleware/treatment/
    getTreatmentsMW.js
    getTreatmentMW.js
    saveTreatmentMW.js
    delTreatmentMW.js


GET /                                   - home: owners and their oldest vaccination
    getDueVaccinationsMW
    renderMW(index.html)

GET /owners                             - owner list
    getOwnersMW
    renderMW(owners.html)

GET,POST /owners/new                    - create a new owner
    saveOwnerMW
    renderMW(owner-new.html)
        - if there was posted content, the form fields are filled with it
        - otherwise they stay empty

GET,POST /owners/:ownerid               - owner details / edit
    getOwnerMW
    saveOwnerMW
    renderMW(owner.html)
        - the owner page shows the owner and its animals

GET /owners/:ownerid/del                - delete an owner
    getOwnerMW
    delOwnerMW
        - removes the owner together with its animals and their treatments
        - redirects to /

GET /animals                            - animal list
    getAnimalsMW
    renderMW(animals.html)

GET,POST /animals/new                   - create a new animal
    saveAnimalMW
    renderMW(animal-new.html)
        - the owner is taken from the referring owner page (:ownerid when present)

GET /animals/:animalid/del              - delete an animal
    getAnimalMW
    delAnimalMW
        - removes the animal together with its treatments
        - redirects to the owner page

GET,POST /animals/:animalid/treatments/new  - add a treatment to an animal
    getAnimalMW
    saveTreatmentMW
    renderMW(animal.html)

GET /animals/:animalid/treatments/:treatmentid/del  - delete a treatment
    getAnimalMW
    getTreatmentMW
    delTreatmentMW
        - redirects to the animal page

GET,POST /animals/:animalid/treatments/:treatmentid  - edit a treatment
    getAnimalMW
    getTreatmentMW
    saveTreatmentMW
    renderMW(animal.html)

GET /animals/:animalid/treatments       - treatments of an animal
    getAnimalMW
    getTreatmentsMW
    renderMW(animal.html)

GET,POST /animals/:animalid             - animal details / edit
    getAnimalMW
    saveAnimalMW
    renderMW(animal.html)
        - the animal page shows the animal and its treatments
